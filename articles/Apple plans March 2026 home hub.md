Device Features and Market Positioning

The home hub, internally codenamed J490 for the tabletop version and J491 for wall-mounted, resembles a "screen-equipped version of the HomePod mini" according to Bloomberg's reporting. Both configurations will include FaceTime cameras and software that recognizes different family members, adjusting available apps and features accordingly.[businesstimes+2](https://www.businesstimes.com.sg/companies-markets/telcos-media-tech/apple-build-upcoming-homepod-screen-tabletop-robot-vietnam)​

Apple originally planned to launch the device in March 2025, but delayed it to wait for an updated version of Siri built on next-generation architecture. The $350 price represents a premium over the full-sized HomePod and is significantly higher than Amazon's competing products, though Apple's operations teams are exploring cost reduction strategies.[macrumors+2](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/10/14/apple-2026-home-hub-cost/)​

The company also plans to release an indoor security camera, codenamed J450, by the end of 2026, and a more advanced tabletop robot with a 9-inch display and motorized arm for 2027. The robot is expected to cost "several hundred dollars" due to its complex components.[businesstimes+1](https://www.businesstimes.com.sg/companies-markets/telcos-media-tech/apple-build-upcoming-homepod-screen-tabletop-robot-vietnam)​

Strategic Manufacturing Shift

The Vietnam manufacturing partnership with BYD marks a notable departure from Apple's typical strategy of building new product categories in China first. BYD will handle final assembly, testing, and packaging for the home devices, continuing Apple's broader effort to reduce dependence on Chinese manufacturing amid ongoing trade tensions.[businesstimes](https://www.businesstimes.com.sg/companies-markets/telcos-media-tech/apple-build-upcoming-homepod-screen-tabletop-robot-vietnam)​

Apple has significantly expanded its Vietnam operations, with 35 supplier facilities in the country as of 2023, making it the company's largest production base in Southeast Asia. The company has invested nearly $16 billion in Vietnam since 2019 and already produces AirPods, iPads, Apple Watches, and some Macs there.[vietnam-briefing+1](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/news/apple-manufacturers-vietnam.html/)​

However, products manufactured in Vietnam still face tariffs under current U.S. trade policies, with a 20% levy on Vietnamese imports. This manufacturing shift comes as President Trump has threatened additional tariffs on Chinese goods and indicated plans to target countries within Chinese supply chains.[bbc+2](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c5ykd997kjjo)​

The home hub launch represents Apple's most serious push into smart home hardware, a market where it has struggled to gain traction against Amazon and Google despite years of HomeKit software development.[appleinsider+1](https://appleinsider.com/articles/24/12/19/how-apples-smart-home-revolution-begins-in-2025)​
