Samsung to unveil Project Moohan XR headset Oct. 21

Samsung officially announced Monday that it will unveil Project Moohan, its long-awaited extended reality headset, at a virtual "Worlds Wide Open" event on October 21 at 10 PM ET. The device represents Samsung's entry into the mixed reality market and will compete directly with Apple's Vision Pro.

The South Korean tech giant confirmed that Project Moohan will be the first commercial product built on Android XR, a new platform co-developed with Google and Qualcomm. Samsung is positioning the headset as an "AI-native device" that will introduce "a new era of multimodal AI" to extended reality experiences.[pcmag+2](https://www.pcmag.com/news/samsung-unveil-its-apple-vision-pro-rival-on-oct-21-how-to-watch)​

Direct Challenge to Apple's Vision Pro

According to recent leaks, the headset—which may be officially branded as Galaxy XR—features dual 4K micro-OLED displays with 4,032 pixels per inch, delivering approximately 29 million total pixels across both eyes. This exceeds Apple's Vision Pro by roughly 6 million pixels while weighing 545 grams compared to the Vision Pro's 600-650 grams.[gsmarena+2](https://www.gsmarena.com/samsungs_vr_headset_project_moohan_leaks_in_full-news-69864.php)​

The Galaxy XR is expected to be powered by Qualcomm's Snapdragon XR2+ Gen 2 chipset and will include both hand tracking and optional physical controllers, differentiating it from Apple's hand-tracking-only approach. Reports suggest the device will be priced between $1,800 and $2,800, significantly undercutting Apple's $3,500 Vision Pro.[mashable+3](https://mashable.com/article/project-moohan-galaxy-vr-apple-vision-pro)​

Early Access and Market Strategy

Samsung opened reservations for the headset Monday, offering early adopters a $100 credit toward additional Samsung products. The company emphasized that reservations carry no purchase obligation, following its typical pre-launch strategy of building anticipation through early sign-ups.[pcmag+2](https://www.pcmag.com/news/samsung-unveil-its-apple-vision-pro-rival-on-oct-21-how-to-watch)​

The timing of Samsung's announcement comes as Apple reportedly shifts focus away from a cheaper Vision Pro model to concentrate on lightweight AR glasses development. Meanwhile, Meta continues to dominate the XR market with over 20 million Quest headsets sold, compared to fewer than 500,000 Vision Pro units reportedly sold by Apple.[technologymagazine+1](https://technologymagazine.com/news/apple-shifts-focus-vision-pro-upgrade-dropped-for-ai-glasse)​

The virtual launch event will stream on Samsung's website and YouTube channel, marking a departure from the company's typical in-person Unpacked events. Samsung expects to reveal pricing, availability, and final specifications during the October 21 presentation, with some reports suggesting initial availability in South Korea before a broader international rollout.[androidcentral+3](https://www.androidcentral.com/gaming/virtual-reality/samsung-worlds-wide-open-event-for-its-xr-project-moohan)​
